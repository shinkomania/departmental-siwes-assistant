About Departmental SIWES Assistant (DSA)

A dedicated system built for Computer Engineering students undertaking a 6-month Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) in Nigeria.

🎯 Mission & Purpose

The Departmental SIWES Assistant (DSA) was conceived to solve two fundamental challenges faced by university engineering students:

  1. Finding High-Quality, Relevant Placements: Moving beyond word-of-mouth by providing transparent, categorized, and verified technology companies, datacenters, and fintech hubs across all Nigerian states.
  2. Mastering SIWES Academic Requirements: Demystifying ITF Form 8, daily logbook entries, supervisor inspection protocols, technical report writing, and final presentation defense.

🛡️ Data Integrity & Source Transparency

A key architectural tenet of DSA is truth in data. The platform strictly distinguishes between:

Verified

Organizations verified by the department or proven to accept engineering interns with active engineering teams.

Online Source

Results returned through structured web research adapters or verified online registries.

Student Submitted

Peer recommendations submitted by fellow students pending formal departmental verification.

🚀 Future AI & Intelligence Capabilities

DSA is architected with modular service boundaries to facilitate upcoming intelligence modules without requiring code rewrites:

  • AI SIWES Chatbot: Interactive Q&A on university guidelines and workplace etiquette.
  • AI Logbook Assistant: Refines draft daily activities into technically precise, academic logbook entries.
  • AI Report Structurer: Analyzes student project notes and generates standard Chapter outlines.
  • Intelligent Placement Matcher: Uses student skill vectors and location proximity for personalized placement matching.
