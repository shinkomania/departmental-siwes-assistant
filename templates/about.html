About DSA

Making SIWES easier to understand and navigate.

Departmental SIWES Assistant is being built as a structured support platform for students and institutions participating in industrial training across Nigeria.

Explore Placements Read SIWES Guide
Core principle

Useful information should remain traceable.

DSA is designed to distinguish where placement information comes from, what has been reviewed, and what still requires confirmation.

The platform

One SIWES support layer, built around real academic structure.

SIWES requirements can differ by institution, department, programme and academic session. DSA is being structured around those differences instead of assuming every student follows the same training arrangement.

Institution-aware

Academic information can be organized from institution to faculty or school, department, programme and SIWES configuration.

Student-centred

Students can build a SIWES profile, discover organizations, save opportunities and keep track of their personal application journey.

Guidance-focused

DSA provides structured SIWES guidance while allowing the platform architecture to support institution and department-specific instructions.

Why DSA exists

SIWES information is often scattered across too many places.

Students may need to understand training requirements, identify suitable organizations, find reliable contact information, track applications and follow instructions from their institution or department.

DSA brings these activities into a more structured workflow while keeping institutional rules and evidence boundaries visible.

Programme-aware by design

SIWES eligibility belongs at programme level.

An institution participating in SIWES does not automatically mean every programme follows the same requirement. DSA's architecture keeps that distinction explicit.

Evidence & transparency

Source, review and current intake are different questions.

DSA avoids treating every organization listing as proof that students are currently being accepted. Placement information should communicate both its origin and what the available evidence actually supports.

Suggest an organization
01 · Source

Where did the information come from?

Examples can include an official organization source, institution placement record, public registry, ITF-related source or community contribution.

02 · Review

Has the contribution been reviewed?

Community suggestions can remain pending while information is assessed instead of immediately appearing as trusted placement data.

03 · Intake

Is there evidence of current SIWES intake?

Previous placements or directory presence do not automatically prove that an organization is accepting students now.

Important platform boundary

DSA supports the SIWES process. It does not replace its authorities.

Departmental SIWES Assistant is designed to complement—not replace—the official requirements, processes and decisions of participating institutions and relevant SIWES authorities. Students should continue to follow the current instructions issued by their institution, department and applicable authorities.
